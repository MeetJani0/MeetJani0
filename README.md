Most AI failures come from **confident guessing**.  
My work focuses on building systems that **retrieve evidence, structure context, and then reason**.

I design AI pipelines where:
- knowledge is explicit  
- uncertainty is controlled  
- outputs are traceable  

This mindset comes from a background in **physics, research, and applied machine learning**.

## 🧭 How I Think

Most AI mistakes are not model failures —  
they are **system design failures**.

I focus on building AI systems where:
- knowledge is **retrieved, not guessed**
- context is **structured before reasoning**
- outputs are **traceable and grounded**

This approach comes from a background in **physics, research**, and **applied machine learning**.
